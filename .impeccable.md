## Design Context

### Users
General visitors discovering a personal project made for fun. They can browse anonymous moments from around the world and casually contribute a whisper, recommendation, photo, journal entry, or activity.

### Brand Personality
Playful, fun, and cute. The experience should feel friendly, expressive, lighthearted, and pleasantly surprising rather than formal or productized.

### Aesthetic Direction
A warm, light scrapbook/editorial world with illustrated objects, tactile paper textures, gentle motion, and charming typography. Preserve the handmade personality and avoid generic SaaS layouts, sterile minimalism, dark technology styling, or overly serious visual language.

### Design Principles
1. Keep discovery delightful: interactions should invite curiosity without obscuring navigation or core actions.
2. Prefer handmade character over generic UI: use the scrapbook illustrations, warm palette, and typographic voice consistently.
3. Let decoration support the experience: playful motion and objects should never block content, keyboard use, readability, or mobile access.
4. Make failure graceful: the visual shell and primary navigation must remain usable when data or third-party services are unavailable.
5. Treat WCAG AA, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and clear focus states as quality baselines while retaining the playful personality.
